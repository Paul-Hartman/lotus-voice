TABLET I - He Who Saw the Deep

He who saw the deep, the foundation of the land,
who knew all things, the wise one,
Gilgamesh, who saw the deep, the foundation of the land,
who knew all things, was wise in all matters.

He explored everywhere,
and learned of everything.
He saw secrets and discovered hidden things.
He brought back a tale of before the Flood.

He went on a distant journey,
pushing himself to exhaustion,
and then he engraved all his toil on a stone tablet.

He built the wall of Uruk-the-Sheepfold,
of holy Eanna, the sacred storehouse.

See its wall, which is like a copper band!
Look at its inner wall, which none can equal!
Touch the stone threshold, which is ancient!

Go up on the wall of Uruk and walk around!
Examine the foundation terrace and inspect the brickwork!
Is not its masonry of kiln-fired brick?
Did not seven wise men lay its foundations?

One square mile is the city,
one square mile is the orchards,
one square mile is the claypits,
as well as the open ground of Ishtar's temple.
Three square miles and the open ground
comprise Uruk.

Look for the copper tablet-box,
undo its bronze lock,
open the lid of its secret,
pick up the tablet of lapis lazuli and read out
the travails of Gilgamesh, all that he went through.

Surpassing all other kings, heroic in stature,
brave scion of Uruk, wild bull on the rampage!
Going at the front, he was the vanguard,
Going at the rear, trusted by his companions,
Mighty net, protector of his people,
Raging flood-wave who destroys even walls of stone!
